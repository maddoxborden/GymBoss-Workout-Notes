How It Works

Simple, on purpose

Join your dorm above using your existing Gym Boss account.

Complete a qualifying activity in Gym Boss — a real lifting session or a real cardio session both count.

Complete 15 active days during your 30-day challenge to flip your Freshman 15.

Keep training — every active day still contributes to your dorm's Dorm Cup score, even after you've flipped your own 15.

Why We're Doing This

This isn't a weight-loss competition

Flip the Freshman 15 isn't about the number on a scale — it's about showing up. Fifteen active days in thirty is realistic for a real semester, and every dorm's total is built from real consistency, not a leaderboard of bodies or calories. It's a reason to actually go, a little campus rivalry, and a community that notices when you show up.
