Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

Camera Access and Usage

We may request access or permission to certain features from your mobile device, specifically your device's camera. This access is strictly used to allow you to scan food barcodes to retrieve nutritional information. We do not capture, record, or transmit photos or videos from your camera to our servers or any third parties.

Geolocation and Background Location

We may request access or permission to track location-based information from your mobile device, either continuously (in the background) or while you are actively using the application. This data is specifically collected to track your cardio routes, distance, and pace, even when your device is locked or the app is minimized.

Personal Data Collection and Deletion

To provide our core features, we collect information you voluntarily provide, including account details, workout logs, and nutrition history. You may permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time directly within the application's settings menu.

AI Processing

When you use "Ask Gym Boss" or get a nutrition estimate, the text of your workout notes, food notes, or questions is sent to our AI provider, OpenAI, to generate a response. We do not send your email address, precise identity, or payment information to OpenAI. See AI Usage for more detail.

Crash Diagnostics and Error Reporting

Gym Boss uses Sentry, a crash and error reporting service, to help us detect and fix app crashes and errors. Crash reports may include device and app state information at the time of the crash, but are not linked to your name, email address, or account identity, and are never used for advertising or tracking.

Purchases and Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Gym Boss Premium, your purchase is processed by Apple or Google directly — we never see or store your card number. Your subscription status is shared with our payments partner, RevenueCat, so the app can unlock Premium features on your account.

Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. Deleting your account through the app removes your profile, workouts, food entries, weigh-ins, and saved AI conversations from our live database. Routine database backups may retain deleted data for a limited additional period before they are cycled out.

Children's Privacy

Gym Boss is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it. Contact us at support@gymbossnotes.com if you believe we have inadvertently collected such information.

Contact for privacy questions: support@gymbossnotes.com
